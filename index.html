Why Added Sugar

Natural sugar is not treated the same way.

knowsugar focuses on added sugar because it is the sugar most people can reduce without avoiding nutrient-rich foods. Whole fruit and plain dairy include fiber, water, protein, vitamins, and minerals — so we do not track them the same way.

GLP-1 Friendly

Added Sugar Tracking Designed for GLP-1 Users

GLP-1 therapy can change appetite, digestion, and how certain foods feel after eating. knowsugar helps you track added sugar by day and by meal — so you can make informed choices that support your goals.

▥

Daily Added Sugar Ceiling

Suggested guide: 30g per day

Track your total added sugar across the day and stay aware of your daily ceiling.

🍴

Per-Meal Pacing Guide

Suggested guide: 10g per meal

Pace added sugar meal by meal so a single sitting does not quietly add up.

♡

Why Only Added Sugar?

knowsugar focuses on added sugar. Natural sugars in whole fruit and plain dairy are not tracked the same way because they come with fiber, water, protein, vitamins, and minerals.
