App information

What the app is, why it matters, and how it helps.

knowsugar gives users a clear explanation of why added sugar awareness matters, what counts as added sugar, and what each feature is designed to do.

What counts as added sugar?

Added sugar means sugar added during processing, preparation, or serving — not the natural sugar found inside whole fruit, plain milk, or other unsweetened whole foods.

  • Examples include table sugar, syrups, soda, sweet coffee drinks, desserts, candy, flavored yogurt, pastries, sauces, cereals, and packaged snacks.
  • The app focuses on added sugar only, so users can track the part of sugar intake that is most hidden in everyday products.

Why track added sugar?

Added sugar can build up quietly through drinks, snacks, desserts, sauces, and packaged foods. knowsugar makes that invisible number visible.

  • Spot repeat sources that push intake higher than expected.
  • Use simple daily tracking as a practical behavior-change tool.
  • Review patterns without tracking every calorie or macro.

Why juice matters behaviorally

Even when juice contains naturally derived sugar, it can behave like a sugary product in real life because much of the fruit’s original fiber structure is removed and the sugar becomes easy to drink quickly.

  • Large amounts can be consumed faster than whole fruit.
  • Juice can feel healthy, so it is easy to underestimate its sugar contribution.
